Conclusions

This project shows that air pollution is still a big problem in Barcelona.

Areas with more traffic and more people usually have higher levels of pollution, especially NO₂ and PM10.

The results also show that pollution can affect people’s health, causing breathing and heart problems.

For this reason, improving air quality is very important for the city and its citizens.

Some actions that could help reduce pollution are:

  • Using public transport
  • Riding bicycles
  • Walking more
  • Creating more green spaces

Although Barcelona has already taken some measures, more actions are still needed.

In conclusion, studying air pollution helps us understand its impact on the environment and on people’s lives, and it is important for creating a healthier and more sustainable city.
